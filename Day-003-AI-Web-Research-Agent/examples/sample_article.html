Why Grounding Matters for AI Agents

Large language models are powerful generators, but they do not automatically know whether a claim is current or supported by a specific source.

Grounding connects model reasoning to external evidence such as webpages, APIs, databases, and retrieved documents.

A useful agent often follows a simple loop: collect evidence with a tool, normalize the result, reason over the evidence, and return an answer that identifies its source.

Developers should also handle failures such as blocked pages, unsupported content types, oversized downloads, and missing API keys.
